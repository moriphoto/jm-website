# Studio stills

Open the media panel:

https://jmceramics.netlify.app/admin/stills.html

That page lists every JPEG already on the site (catalogue pieces and About / Courses pictures). Tick what you want. Open in Stills. Process. Download 1920 × 1080. Come back here and replace the image on the piece.

Do not upload raw phone frames to the public catalogue. Sit them on the cove first.
