About

Born and raised in Cornwall. The forms are a response to that coast, and to the kiln.

John completed a degree in ceramics at Harrow in 2001.

He develops his own clays, glazes and slips to respond to flame, ember and ash.

The Gweek kiln

In 2020 he built a 140 cubic-foot anagama at Gweek.

Loading an anagama is said to be the hardest part of the firing.

Where a pot sits decides what it becomes.
